### **Grant Proposal Research Checklist**

### **1\. Define Your Project**

**Clarify Goals and Objectives:**

- Identify the problem or need your project addresses.
- Outline clear, measurable objectives and desired outcomes.

**Determine Your Target Audience:**

- Define who will benefit from the project and how.

### **2\. Identify Funding Opportunities**

**Search for Relevant Grants:**

- Use grant databases (e.g., Grants.gov, Foundation Center).
- Check professional networks and industry-specific sources.

**Match Funding Priorities:**

- Ensure the grant’s focus aligns with your project’s mission.

**Note Application Deadlines:**

- Create a timeline with key dates for each potential grant.

### **3\. Understand Eligibility and Requirements**

**Review Eligibility Criteria:**

- Confirm that your organization and project meet the necessary requirements.

**Examine Application Guidelines:**

- Read all instructions carefully to understand format, content, and documentation needed.

**Identify Special Conditions:**

- Note any specific criteria such as geographical focus, target demographics, or thematic restrictions.

### **4\. Analyze the Grant Guidelines**

**Break Down the Proposal Structure:**

- List required sections (e.g., executive summary, project narrative, budget, timelines).

**Highlight Evaluation Criteria:**

- Identify the metrics and priorities the reviewers will focus on.

**Document Submission Instructions:**

- Understand the format (e.g., online submission, paper application) and required attachments.

### **5\. Research the Funding Organization**

**Review Mission and Values:**

- Familiarize yourself with the funding body’s goals and priorities.

**Study Past Grants:**

- Look at previously funded projects to gain insight into what the funder values.

**Identify Key Contacts:**

- Find program officers or contact points for any clarifications.

### **6\. Gather Supporting Data and Materials**

**Collect Relevant Research and Statistics:**

- Include data that underscores the need for your project.

**Compile Organizational Information:**

- Prepare your organization’s history, success stories, and key personnel bios.

**Draft a Preliminary Budget:**

- Outline anticipated expenses, funding needs, and potential revenue streams.

### **7\. Consult and Network**

**Reach Out to Colleagues/Mentors:**

- Seek advice from individuals with successful grant experience.

**Attend Workshops or Webinars:**

- Participate in events that can offer additional insights into the proposal process.

**Contact the Funder:**

- Don’t hesitate to ask questions if any guidelines are unclear.

### **8\. Organize and Plan Your Proposal**

**Develop a Proposal Outline:**

- Sketch a draft structure that meets all requirements.

**Create a Timeline:**

- Set milestones for drafting, reviewing, and finalizing the proposal.

**Assign Responsibilities:**

- Delegate sections or tasks among team members if applicable.

### **9\. Final Review Preparation**

**Review Alignment:**

- Ensure every section of your proposal aligns with both your project’s goals and the funder’s priorities.

**Seek Feedback:**

- Have colleagues or mentors review your draft for clarity, coherence, and completeness.

**Proofread and Edit:**

- Conduct thorough checks for typos, formatting errors, and compliance with guidelines.

### **10\. Prepare for Submission**

**Double-Check Submission Requirements:**

- Confirm file formats, page limits, and any other technical details.

**Submit Early if Possible:**

- Aim to finish the proposal well before the deadline to allow time for unexpected issues.

**Document the Submission:**

- Keep copies of all submitted materials and confirmation of receipt.
