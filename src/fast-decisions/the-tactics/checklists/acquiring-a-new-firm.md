Does the firm have strong relationships with reputable nonprofits?

Assess client concentration - are they stable, with long-term contracts?

Does it have specialized expertise and/or sector focus?

Does it have high owner dependence? (Red flag)

Does it have a capable bench of people who want to stay?

Does it have values alignment and culture fit?

Does it have clean financials?

Does it rely on 1-2 big clients/events? (Red flag)

Does it help you extend your geographic reach?

Does it help you penetrate a new vertical?

Does it have positive cashflow?

Does it have no/low debt?

Does it allow service expansion?

Does it allow cross-selling?

Does it allow for talent acquisition?

Does it eliminate a rival?

Does it allow us to consolidate back-office functions?

Does it allow acquisition of IP?
