I was behind a young Kenyan girl taking her first airplane ride. She told her father:

_Dad? This is so beautiful.  
We are cutting through the clouds.   
Ooh shaky!  
Dad! Dad? Dad?  
How do clouds taste?  
I cannot even see anyone._

I love this idea - the boundless curiosity of the present moment, and the electric anticipation of what's to come. "How Do Clouds Taste?" is an exploration of dreams and their manifestation. It's a collection of moments that journey into the depths of our dreams and ambitions.

In this moment of my life, I desperately wanted to move forward toward the future. I also felt the bitterness that comes with an ending. In these photographs, I acknowledge both feelings and their paradox. Inside the contradiction, there's a little voice inside me wondering how the clouds taste.
